Travel Style Guide

Find the exact bag configuration tailored to your travel itinerary.

Business Travel Style

Recommended Luggage Type: Compact Cabin Spinner & Laptop Backpack

Useful Features: Front-opening suit compartments, TSA locks, power bank pockets.

Packing Suggestions: Keep documents and electronics accessible in an external backpack pocket while storing crease-sensitive suits in a structured spinner.

Family Travel Style

Recommended Luggage Type: 3-Piece Matching Luggage Set

Useful Features: Expandable zipper compartments, 360-degree dual spinner wheels.

Packing Suggestions: Assign specific color bags or organizers per family member for organized unpacking at your resort destination.

Weekend Trips Style

Recommended Luggage Type: Canvas or Leather Duffle / Weekender Bag

Useful Features: Detachable shoulder straps, shoe compartments.

Packing Suggestions: Roll casual clothes tight to maximize space and reduce creasing for quick 2-to-3 day getaways.

Long Holidays Style

Recommended Luggage Type: Large Hard-Shell Spinner Case

Useful Features: High-impact polycarbonate shell, interior dividers.

Packing Suggestions: Utilize packing cubes to group attire by week or activity type to keep large suitcases organized.

International Travel Style

Recommended Luggage Type: Medium Spinner & Secure Travel Organizer

Useful Features: Integrated security lock systems, reinforced corners.

Packing Suggestions: Ensure carry-on bags meet airline overhead dimensions and keep travel documentation secure in a dedicated pouch.

Everyday Travel Style

Recommended Luggage Type: Lightweight Commuter Backpack

Useful Features: Water-resistant fabrics, ergonomic back support, quick-access pockets.

Packing Suggestions: Perfect for urban commutes, day excursions, and carrying daily essential devices comfortably.

How To Choose The Right Luggage

Key factors to evaluate when selecting your next piece of travel gear.

Size

Ensure your dimensions match trip duration and airline carry-on allowances.

Weight

Opt for lightweight frames to maximize your actual packing allowance.

Material

Choose between rigid polycarbonates for impact protection or soft nylon for flexibility.

Wheels

360-degree double spinner wheels offer smoother gliding across terminal floors.

Handle

Look for sturdy, multi-stage telescopic aluminum handles for height adjustments.

Compartments

Internal mesh dividers and straps keep clothing structured during transit.

Security

Integrated combination locks provide additional peace of mind for checked baggage.

Travel Frequency

Frequent flyers benefit from high-density fabrics and heavy-duty zippers.
